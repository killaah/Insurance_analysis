**Insurance Data Analysis Project**

**1. Business Objective**

To analyse an insurance claims dataset to identify trends in claim frequency, premium distribution, and segments associated with higher average claim costs. The goal was to simulate how structured data analysis can support underwriting and risk evaluation decisions.

**2. Tools Used**

Microsoft Excel

Pivot Tables

Data filtering and sorting

Basic data cleaning

Charts and visualisations

**3. Key Insights**

Identified variations in average claim amounts across insurance policy types

Observed differences in claim frequency across customer demographic segments

Compared total premiums collected versus total claims paid to evaluate loss patterns

Highlighted segments with higher average claim costs that may indicate increased risk exposure

**4. Business Relevance**

This project demonstrates the ability to extract insights from structured insurance data to support risk assessment, portfolio evaluation, and evidence-based decision-making. The analysis mirrors the type of structured evaluation required in underwriting and business analysis environments.

**5. What I Learned**

Through this project, I strengthened my ability to:

Segment and analyse structured datasets

Use pivot tables to summarise large datasets efficiently

Translate numerical outputs into clear business insights

Present findings visually to support stakeholder understanding
